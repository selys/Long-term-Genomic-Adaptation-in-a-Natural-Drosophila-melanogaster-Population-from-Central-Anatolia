Is pairwise FST time-dependent? Mantel test
=============================================

Cohorts: 25 (2015_09 excluded)
Valid pairwise comparisons: 300 

Mantel test, PEARSON (linear relationship):
  Observed Mantel r = 0.1585
  Permutation p-value (n=9,999, one-sided) = 0.0612

Mantel test, SPEARMAN (rank-based; more appropriate if the pattern is
peak-driven / non-linear rather than a smooth monotonic increase):
  Observed Mantel rho = 0.2371
  Permutation p-value (n=9,999, one-sided) = 0.0033

Descriptive regression (FST ~ temporal distance in years) -- for effect
size / interpretability only; the Mantel p-values above are the valid
significance tests given non-independence among pairwise FST values:
  slope = 0.00114 FST per year of separation
  R^2 = 0.0251
